About the Programme

Armour Community Training is run by Armour Training, a non-profit organisation, supported by The National Lottery.

We offer free sessions for the local community in the Borough of Harrow. Train in a welcoming, encouraging and nurturing learning environment. Relieve stress, learn to keep safe and control aggression in a calm, disciplined and family-focused environment. Improve your mental and physical health.
