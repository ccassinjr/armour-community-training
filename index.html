Fridays

Inclusive Free Boxing Sessions

Specially designed for young people with ADHD, Autism, and SEND.

Safe. Supported. Respected. You belong.

A safe, supportive, and structured environment to build focus, fitness, confidence, and social connection.

Led by experienced, DBS-checked coaches with an understanding of neurodiverse young people.

  • Small group environment
  • Sensory-aware coaching approach
  • No previous boxing experience needed
  • Parents/carers welcome to stay initially if helpful
