About the Programme

Fitness for Everyone in the Community

Good fitness coaching shouldn't cost a fortune. Armour Academy has been awarded National Lottery funding to bring free fitness and conditioning classes to the people of Harrow.

These sessions take place inside our fully equipped boxing and fitness club, the same one used by our athletes and PT clients every day. Professional equipment, experienced coaches, no cost.

Whether you're new to fitness or just getting back into it, the sessions are welcoming and the coaching is solid at every level.

About Armour Academy

Harrow's Home of Boxing & Fitness

Armour Academy is a boxing and fitness club based in Harrow, London. We run boxing, fitness conditioning, and personal training for everyone from complete beginners to competitive fighters.

Our gym at 37 The Arches has proper training equipment, coaches who know their stuff, and a community that backs every member. The National Lottery funded programme is our way of opening the doors to the wider Harrow community.
